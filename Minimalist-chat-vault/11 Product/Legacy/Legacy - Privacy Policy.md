# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

To provide you with a seamless chat experience, we collect the following information:

- **Account Data:** Email address, display name, and authentication tokens provided by Google or Email sign-up.
- **Chat Data:** Messages, uploaded images, and reactions sent within the app.
- **Billing Data:** Subscription status and Stripe Customer IDs (we do not store raw credit card numbers).

## 2. Third-Party Services

We utilize industry-standard, secure infrastructure to power Minimalist Chat:

- **Google Firebase:** Used for secure user authentication, real-time database hosting, and cloud storage for your images.
- **Stripe:** Used to securely process subscription payments. Your payment details are handled directly by Stripe.

## 3. Your Right to Deletion

You have complete control over your data. You can permanently delete your account at any time via the Settings menu. Doing so will instantly erase your user profile and authentication records from our database.

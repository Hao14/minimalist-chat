## Download FAQ

Not yet. The web app is the production path right now, and native apps are planned.

Yes. Open the web app on mobile, then add it to your Home Screen for an app-like flow.

Yes. Base is free; Advanced and Pro add higher limits, analytics, video, badges, and power features.

### Will native apps sync with the web app?

Yes. The goal is one account and the same rooms across web, desktop, and mobile.

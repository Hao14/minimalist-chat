# Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

## What is Minimalist Chat?

Minimalist Chat is a real-time messaging app built around private rooms. Every room is more than a chat feed — it has a dynamic homepage, collaborative documents, and a shared whiteboard so your group can talk, plan, and create in one place.

## How do I create or join a room?

Open the room sidebar and use **Create** to start a new room, or **Join** to enter one with an invite link or code. Room creators get a sharable invite link from the room menu, and can manage members, rules, resources, and events from the room's Home tab.

## What are Docs and the Whiteboard?

Each room includes **Collaborative Docs** — lightweight documents that update live for everyone in the room — and a **Shared Whiteboard** with draggable sticky notes for brainstorming. Both sync in real time, so changes appear instantly for every member.

## What do the Advanced and Pro tiers include?

Base accounts can upload 10 MB files up to 500 MB/day, use custom accent themes, and create up to 3 rooms. **Advanced** raises that to 700 MB files, 1.5 GB/day, and 5 created rooms with a tier badge. **Pro** raises it to 3 GB files, 9 GB/day, unlimited rooms, room analytics, video calls, offline viewing, a personal AI agent, and a Pro badge.

## How do I add friends and send private messages?

Open the Contacts panel to search users by name or short ID, send friend requests, and start private chats. You'll get a notification and a title-bar alert whenever someone messages you.

## Is my data private?

Your account and messages are handled according to our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). You can delete your account and associated profile data at any time from Settings.
